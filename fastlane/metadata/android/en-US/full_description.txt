ExpenSage is a frictionless expense tracker companion. Log what you spent in a couple of taps, and see where your money goes.

Features:
* Quick add from the home-screen widget, quick settings tile, or a pinned shortcut
* Works offline: expenses are queued on the device and synced automatically
* Double-submit protection with idempotency keys
* Today and month summaries, category breakdown, spending trend, and insights
* Daily reminder so you never forget to log

ExpenSage works with your own ExpenSage server. Import your setup link to connect. The app contains no ads, no trackers, and no Google services.
